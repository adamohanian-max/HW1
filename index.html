Welcome to UML GC

Alongside the Merrimack River, Umass Lowell's 3-hole golf course offers students an opportunity to enjoy a short round of golf on campus. With a good mix of distances, players will have the opportunity to test their skills in a variety of ways.

Hole 1 — "The River Bend"

Par 4, 350 yards. This hole features a gentle dogleg left around the river, with a bunker guarding the green on the right. With the river guarding the left side of the fairway and trees to the right, this seemingly easy par 4 can lead to a sneakily challenging experience.

Hole 2 — "The Valley"

Par 3, 150 yards. This hole takes advantage of the natural valley, offering a scenic route to the green. With bunkers on the left and right of the hole, precision is key to walk away with a par.

Hole 3 — "The Summit"

Par 5, 500 yards. The most challenging hole on the course, featuring a long approach shot to a raised green. With bunkers guarding the front of the green, and a steep incline behind it, this hole can be reward players who play conservatively.
